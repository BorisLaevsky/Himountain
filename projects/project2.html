Being Different

12.9.24 - 14.9.24

It is about the difference between watching a coming-of-age film while coming of age and watching a coming-of-age film after you have come of age, and it is located in an area known for late nights of several youth groups, where freaks, emos, chavs, punks, and other children of divorced parents are being different, but together.
