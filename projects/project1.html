The Weekend

6.6.24 - 8.6.24

The Weekend took place in a historical home imported entirely by ship from Maine, USA to Israel.

At the end of the 19th century, a group of over 150 Evangelist Americans from Maine decided to follow their faith and vision and settle in the Holy Land. Led by George Adams, these families made a 42-day journey overseas and landed on the beach between Tel Aviv and Jaffa. They brought with them their New England style homes. Sick of standing aside, they started accelerating the prophecy of their faith. Telling a story or making one … potato, potahto. Evangelists, like us, understand that faith is not only about waiting. It can also be about moving the world forward, until reality catches up.

The title presents the duration. There is a repetitive horror-movie plotline: a group of youngsters spending a weekend in an old house. A place, a ghost, a trace, some unsolved personal business… decorated with quasi-historical periods… and you know what happens next. But history is a movie too. The Maine house was in a way always a film set, walking through that historical house, you disbelieve its own origin.

Stories exist only when they are being told.

They have no history.
