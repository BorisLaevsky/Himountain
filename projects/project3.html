Down to Earth

5.6.25 - 12.6.25 - 14.6.25

Park Hayarkon is one of the few places in Tel Aviv where something like nature (however reduced) still exists. We wanted to simulate the feeling of discovering a natural escape within an urban area: a scene of slow revelation, where the viewer, as protagonist, finds something hidden.

Tommy's cute animal mysterious wayfinding signs lead the protagonist through the park, until they find Naama's 00.01 Rest , lifting up their head a bit they should spot Iliya's Dove (dear reltoids) , hidden between 3 trees. Then, a path, going down to the water, Boris's Cyrano de Bergerac .

Then, Anna's hat, World Domination and Silver Dining in the dry bushes.

Then, Adi's Dolphin leaned on one of the bridge's plinths. Boris's Kaspar Hauser in front of the lake view, exactly in the middle of the island. Iliya's poppies in the Wind , looking at Kaspar.

Anna's exhibition text on a small stump, held with a small rock, looking at Kaspar.
